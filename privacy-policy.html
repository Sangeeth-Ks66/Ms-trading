1. Business-to-Business (B2B) Scope & Operation

M.S TRADING COMPANY operates exclusively as a wholesale supplier of sanitary fittings, flat glass, hand tools, builders hardware, and construction supplies. We provide bulk quotation, invoice, and logistics coordination services to registered business entities, institutional builders, civil engineering contractors, and hardware retail dealers. We do not operate an open retail consumer shopping cart on this website.
