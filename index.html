About M.S TRADING COMPANY

M.S TRADING COMPANY is a premier wholesale supplier based in Malappuram, Kerala, dedicated to serving civil contractors, real estate developers, and hardware retail merchants with consistent, high-standard construction and building supplies.

Established in November 2016 as a registered Partnership firm, we maintain robust inventory pipelines for sanitary fittings, flat glass sheets, commercial hand tools, and builders hardware. We are fully compliant with Indian Goods and Services Tax (GST) regulations, ensuring seamless B2B wholesale tax credit invoicing on every bulk shipment.

Our centralized distribution facility at Inkel Greens, Panakkad, Oorakam provides rapid fulfillment and logistics connectivity throughout Northern Kerala and neighboring commercial corridors.
